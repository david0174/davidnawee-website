ABOUT ME

Progress over perfection.Confidence through practice.

I’m David Nawee – English teacher and native speaker, born and raised in the US and based in Hannover. I help people of every level speak English with confidence: from their very first words to job interviews and business meetings.

I learned a new language as an adult myself, so I know how it feels to search for words and worry about mistakes. That’s why every lesson is built around what you need – speaking practice, grammar, writing or exam preparation – with honest feedback and a lot of practice.

Between lessons you can practice for free on my own learning platform, learn.nawee.net – with listening and speaking exercises, picture cards and games.
