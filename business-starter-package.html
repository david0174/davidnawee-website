DATA PROTECTION

Privacy built in from the start

Anyone collecting data through a website – via contact form, booking, newsletter or payment – must follow the GDPR and, in Germany, the TDDDG. I set up your systems so that data protection is part of the solution from day one:

✓ Only necessary data

Forms only ask for what you really need – nothing more.

✓ Providers with a contract

Where possible, providers with EU servers and a data processing agreement (DPA).

✓ No unnecessary tracking

Cookie-free visitor statistics where possible – a cookie banner only where it’s really needed.

✓ Newsletter with double opt-in

Sign-up with a confirmation link and an unsubscribe link in every email.

✓ Encrypted

HTTPS for the whole website, secure passwords and access.

✓ Documented

An overview of which services process which data – as the basis for your privacy policy.

This is how I work myself: my own websites use cookie-free statistics, double opt-in and providers with data processing agreements. The legal review of your privacy policy and legal notice is done by a lawyer or a reputable generator – I don’t replace that.
