⚖️ Supporting AI literacy is a requirement

Since February 2, 2025, the EU AI Act (Article 4) has required companies that use AI to take measures for their staff’s AI literacy. Since July 2026, it reads: companies must take measures to support the development of AI literacy. A practical beginner workshop is exactly that kind of measure.

Please note: this is not legal advice – which measures you need is best clarified with your legal or data protection advisor.
