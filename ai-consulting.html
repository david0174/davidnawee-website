AI CONSULTING · HANNOVER & ONLINE

AI consulting: where AI really helps you

Together we find out where AI can genuinely save your business time, money and effort – and I make sure the solution works in everyday life.

It’s not about knowing as many AI tools as possible. It’s about connecting what the technology can do with your actual tasks – for companies, teams and freelancers. Clear, practical and without jargon.

Free initial call → AI training for teams →

WHAT YOU GET

A trainer shows what AI can do. A consultant finds out what you need it for.

Before we talk about tools, we look at how you work: which tasks take a lot of time, repeat themselves or are more complicated than they need to be? That’s where we start – and at the end we measure whether it pays off.

HOW I WORK

Six steps – from the question to the result

1. Understand your workflows

I talk to you and your team to find the tasks that take the most time. Example: 50 similar customer inquiries every day.

2. Find the AI potential

Together we decide where AI makes sense – and where it doesn’t. Not everything that’s possible is worth it.

3. Choose the right tools

I compare providers and recommend what fits your business, your budget and your data.

4. Implement

Set up tools, connect applications and – where it makes sense – automate workflows.

5. Train your team

Everyone learns to use AI effectively: good prompts, checking results, protecting data.

6. Measure the results

Does a workflow really save two hours a week? Is the error rate going down? We check – and adjust.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. You bring your knowledge of your business – I translate the technology. Everything is explained clearly.

Do I need expensive new software?

Usually not. Often the tools you already have (e.g. Microsoft 365) or affordable subscriptions are enough. We start small and only expand when it pays off.

How much does AI consulting cost?

It depends on the scope – from a compact potential analysis to a guided implementation. In the free initial call we clarify what you need; then you get a clear quote. No VAT is charged under the German small business rule (§ 19 UStG).

Who is this for?

Small and medium-sized businesses, teams and freelancers – on site in and around Hannover or online across Germany and beyond.
