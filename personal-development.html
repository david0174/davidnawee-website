FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between coaching and therapy?

Coaching is for people who are coping well in everyday life and want to develop. It looks forward – at goals and next steps. For mental illness, therapy is the right path.

How many sessions do I need?

It varies a lot. Sometimes one or two conversations bring clarity; for bigger changes a package is worth it.

Is the coaching confidential?

Yes. Everything you share in coaching stays between us.

Is coaching also available in German?

Yes – in English or German, whichever you prefer.
