OFFER · COMMUNICATION COACHING

Communication Coaching

Say what you mean. Be understood. Speak with confidence.

Many people – native speakers included – know exactly what they think, but hold back, ramble or can't find the right words when it matters. Communication Coaching helps you speak up with confidence and communicate clearly in meetings, presentations and difficult conversations.

For native and fluent English speakers · online and in person in Hannover.

TOPICS

What we work on

Confidence & Speaking Up

  • Saying what you think and feel
  • Speaking up in meetings
  • Less hedging, fewer apologies
  • Staying calm under pressure

Clarity & Structure

  • Leading with your main point
  • Explaining complex ideas simply
  • Structuring answers on the spot
  • Clear, concise emails

Difficult Conversations

  • Giving constructive feedback
  • Disagreeing respectfully
  • Setting boundaries & saying no
  • Handling conflict calmly

Presentations & Public Speaking

  • Structure that sticks
  • Voice, pace & body language
  • Managing nerves
  • Handling questions

Leading Meetings

  • Focused agendas & clear outcomes
  • Getting everyone to contribute
  • Decisions & follow-up

Communicating Across Language Barriers

  • Speaking clearly for non-native colleagues
  • Avoiding idioms without talking down
  • Checking understanding
  • Leading international teams
