ABOUT ME

Progress over perfection.Confidence through practice.

I’m David Nawee, English coach – born and raised in the US. For me, learning a language is about more than knowing the right words. It’s about having the confidence to express yourself and take part in conversations without being held back by the fear of making mistakes.

In my coaching, we focus on practical speaking, constructive feedback and applying what you learn to real-life situations, at work and in everyday conversations. My training as a certified life coach helps me understand what holds people back – and how to build confidence step by step.

You don’t need to be perfect before you start speaking. I know this from experience: I learned German as an adult and became business fluent in less than three years – making plenty of mistakes along the way.

I also studied computer science in the Bridge program at the NYU Tandon School of Engineering. There I built a solid understanding of computer theory and programming and developed my web development skills further. That’s why I build my websites and learning tools myself – for example nawee.net, my free English learning platform.

How I work

We practice your real situations: your next meeting, a client call, your presentation – not textbook dialogues.

Example: Meeting the London team on Thursday? Then we rehearse it beforehand – with your topics and the phrases you actually need.

Try it free: lesson “Mission Meeting” (in German) →

Every coaching program starts where you are: your level, your goals and your situations.

Example: One person needs confidence in small talk, another needs precision in negotiations – that’s why there’s no standard plan.

Find out where you stand: Communication Check →

Complex topics in small, clear steps – with explanations in German where they help.

Example: Instead of grammar tables, you learn how to ask follow-up questions politely – with ready-to-use phrases.

See a sample lesson (in German): “Asking follow-up questions” →

I show you how to use AI tools wisely to practice between sessions – without becoming dependent on them.

Example: You write an email yourself, then have an AI tool check it and explain your mistakes – so you learn from the difference.
