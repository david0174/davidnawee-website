ABOUT ME

From idea to everyday practice.Technology, business sense and people skills.

I’m David Nawee – AI & business consultant and communication coach, born and raised in the US and based in Hannover. I help businesses and the people in them use AI where it really helps, run their business with more clarity and communicate with impact.

I built Nawee myself: the website, booking, payments, newsletter and a free learning platform – much of it with AI tools. That’s how I know what AI can and can’t do in practice, and what it really takes to set up and run a small business.

As a certified life coach I also know the other side: new tools and processes only work when people understand them and feel confident using them. That’s why I always work with the people, not just the technology.

My approach: progress over perfection. We start small, test what works and build from there – on your real tasks, not in theory. Whether you want to bring AI into your team, sharpen your business or speak with more confidence, we start where you are and take the next step together.
