FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to book all six steps?

No. You start where you are – some people only need the setup checklist, others only a website and AI tools.

What does it cost?

The first call is free. The business starter package has its own page; everything else gets a clear quote after our call. No VAT is charged under the German small business rule (§ 19 UStG).

Do you do my taxes or register my business for me?

No. I explain the steps and prepare you with checklists; you register yourself. For tax and legal questions I bring in a qualified tax adviser or lawyer.

I’m new to Germany – can we work in English?

Yes. I’m a native English speaker and know how confusing German paperwork can be at first.

Online or in person?

Both: in and around Hannover in person, or online across Germany.
