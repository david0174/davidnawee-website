Business consulting: clear processes, a sharper offer, steady growth

For small and medium-sized businesses and freelancers who want to grow – without getting lost in day-to-day work.

Most businesses don’t need a 100-page strategy. They need an outside view, clear priorities and someone who helps put the changes into practice. That’s what I do – practical, step by step and without jargon.

WHAT YOU GET

An outside view – and help with the implementation

We look at how your business really works today: how inquiries come in, how quotes are made, what you sell at what price and where time gets lost. Then we decide together what to change first.

SIX AREAS

Where I help your business grow

1. Processes & workflows

We map how work flows through your business and remove bottlenecks, double work and manual steps. Example: inquiries that get lost between email, phone and WhatsApp.

2. Offer & positioning

What exactly do you sell, to whom and at what price? Together we sharpen your offer into clear packages that are easy to explain and easy to buy.

3. Customers & sales

From the first contact to the repeat client: inquiries, quotes, follow-up and reviews – a simple process that doesn’t depend on your memory.

4. Digital presence

Website, online booking, Google Business Profile and newsletter – so customers find you and can book you without back-and-forth.

5. AI-ready business

Where can AI take work off your plate? I combine business consulting with AI consulting – processes first, then the right tools.

6. Growth plan

A 90-day plan with clear priorities, responsibilities and a few numbers that show whether it’s working.

TYPICAL SITUATIONS

What we often work on

Example: a small business with five to twenty employees.

The situationA possible approach
Inquiries get lost between email, phone and messages→ One inquiry process with a simple form, booking or CRM
Quotes take days→ Templates and a standard quote process – with AI drafts where it fits
Prices haven’t changed in years→ Price check and clear packages instead of one-off quotes
Nobody knows exactly who does what→ Clear roles, checklists and shared documentation
The website brings hardly any inquiries→ A clear message, a visible call to action and a Google profile
Growth plans only exist in your head→ A written 90-day plan with priorities and simple metrics

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is business consulting for?

Small and medium-sized businesses, teams and freelancers – on site in and around Hannover or online across Germany and beyond.

Do I need to change everything at once?

No. We start with the one or two changes that bring the most, and build from there.

How much does business consulting cost?

It depends on the scope – from a compact business check to a guided implementation. In the free initial call we clarify what you need; then you get a clear quote. No VAT is charged under the German small business rule (§ 19 UStG).

How is this different from AI consulting?

Business consulting starts with your processes, offer and customers. AI consulting focuses on where AI tools can help. Many clients combine both.

How do we start?

With a free 15-minute call. You tell me where your business stands and what should change – and we find the biggest lever.
