FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the coaching in English or German?

Both are possible. Many clients switch too – for example preparing in German and rehearsing in English.

How many sessions do I need?

For one specific situation, one to three sessions are often enough. For longer-term development, such as a new leadership role, a package is worth it.

Is the coaching confidential?

Yes. Everything you share in coaching stays between us.

Can my employer pay for the coaching?

Of course. If you like, I’ll invoice your company.
