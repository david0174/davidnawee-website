Nawee Learning – a free English learning platform

Problem

Learners need practice between lessons – but good free resources are scattered everywhere.

Solution

  • Courses with lessons, listening practice and a placement test
  • 100 picture cards with spaced repetition
  • Reading texts, typing and rhyme games, an account with progress
  • Fully in German and English

Result

A public, free platform – live and run by me.

Tools: HTML/CSS/JavaScript · Supabase · Web Audio · Claude

Personal finance dashboard

Problem

Money spread across banks, brokers and crypto, in euros and dollars – no overview, and no wish to hand bank logins to a third party.

Solution

  • Live prices for stocks, ETFs and crypto, EUR/USD conversion
  • Bank statement import, spending by category, recurring costs
  • Flags money earning 0% – compared with low-risk benchmarks
  • Wealth goal, Roth IRA limits and price alerts

Result

A complete overview – running only on my own computer, with read-only access.

Tools: Python · SQLite · finance APIs (ECB, US Treasury, Finnhub) · Claude

Radio transcripts & vocabulary lists

Problem

Listening to German radio, many words slip by – I wanted to read exactly what was said.

Solution

  • Records a news station every hour
  • Transcribes everything word for word, offline (speech recognition)
  • Filters out repeated news items
  • Builds a daily page with search and a vocabulary list (with der/die/das)

Result

A searchable transcript and new vocabulary every day – for private use only, deleted automatically after two days.

Tools: Python · Whisper (speech recognition) · spaCy · Claude

Searchable screenshot archive

Problem

Hundreds of screenshots of words and situations I wanted to remember – but nothing could be found again.

Solution

  • Reads the text in every screenshot (macOS text recognition)
  • One page with all screenshots, sorted by month
  • Instant search with highlighting

Result

Every saved word can be found again in seconds – no cloud.

Tools: Swift (macOS Vision) · Python · Claude
